Company Policy

Working Hours:
Employees must work from 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Friday.

Leave Policy:
Employees receive 12 casual leaves and 12 sick leaves per year.

Work From Home:
Employees can work from home two days per week with manager approval.

Salary:
Employee salaries are credited on the last working day of every month.

Holiday Policy:
Employees receive paid holidays on officially declared public holidays.
